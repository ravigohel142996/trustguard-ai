Common Scam Patterns and Red Flags

Job Scams:
- Legitimate employers never ask for payment upfront for job opportunities
- Real companies don't ask for money for background checks or training materials
- Be wary of job offers that seem too good to be true with minimal requirements
- Verify company credentials through official websites
- Never share personal banking details during initial recruitment

Phishing Attempts:
- Banks never ask for passwords or PINs via email or SMS
- Official organizations use proper email domains, not free email services
- Look for spelling errors and grammatical mistakes in official-looking messages
- Verify URLs before clicking - hover to see actual destination
- Real companies don't create urgency to bypass your judgment

Investment Frauds:
- No legitimate investment guarantees returns
- High returns with low risk is a major red flag
- Ponzi schemes promise unrealistic profits
- Verify investment advisors with regulatory authorities
- Never invest based solely on social media recommendations

Lottery and Prize Scams:
- You cannot win a lottery you didn't enter
- Real lotteries don't ask for fees to claim prizes
- Legitimate organizations don't demand immediate payment
- Winners are notified through official channels, not random messages
- Tax obligations are handled after receiving prizes, not before

Safe Communication Practices:
- Verify sender identity through official channels
- Don't share OTPs or passwords with anyone
- Use two-factor authentication when available
- Keep software and security updates current
- Report suspicious activities to authorities
