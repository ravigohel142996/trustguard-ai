Safe Business Practices and Legitimate Communications

Legitimate Business Communications:
- Professional companies use official email domains
- Proper grammar and spelling in all communications
- Clear contact information and physical addresses provided
- Transparent terms and conditions
- No pressure tactics or artificial urgency
- Respect for customer privacy and data protection

Authentic Government Communications:
- Official government emails come from .gov.in domains
- Government agencies don't demand immediate payments via phone
- Proper identification and reference numbers provided
- Information available on official government portals
- Multiple channels for verification available
- Clear escalation procedures for complaints

Genuine Financial Institutions:
- Banks communicate through verified channels
- Proper security protocols in place
- Never ask for sensitive information via unsecured channels
- Transparent fee structures
- Regulated by Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- Physical branches and verified customer service
- Proper grievance redressal mechanisms

E-commerce Safety:
- Verified seller ratings and reviews
- Secure payment gateways (HTTPS)
- Clear return and refund policies
- Trackable shipping information
- Customer protection policies
- Authentic product descriptions
- Transparent pricing

Digital Payment Safety:
- UPI transactions are secure and instant
- Never share UPI PIN or OTP
- Verify merchant details before payment
- Use trusted payment apps only
- Check transaction history regularly
- Report suspicious transactions immediately
- Enable transaction alerts
