Common Scam Categories in India

1. Job and Employment Scams:
Job scams involve fraudsters posing as legitimate employers or recruitment agencies. They typically ask for upfront payment for registration, training, or processing fees. Genuine companies never charge candidates for job applications or interviews. Red flags include promises of high salaries for minimal work, requests for payment before job confirmation, and lack of proper company documentation.

2. Lottery and Prize Scams:
These scams claim you've won a lottery, contest, or prize that you never entered. Scammers ask you to pay taxes, processing fees, or customs charges to claim your "winnings." Remember: legitimate lotteries deduct fees from the prize amount and never ask winners to pay upfront. If you didn't buy a ticket or enter a contest, you can't win it.

3. Tech Support Scams:
Fraudsters pose as technical support representatives from well-known companies like Microsoft, Apple, or your internet service provider. They claim your computer has a virus or security issue and offer to fix it remotely. They may ask for remote access to your computer or payment for unnecessary services. Legitimate tech companies don't make unsolicited calls about computer problems.

4. Romance and Dating Scams:
Scammers create fake profiles on dating sites or social media to build romantic relationships. After gaining trust, they ask for money for emergencies, travel expenses, or investments. They often claim to be overseas and unable to meet in person. Never send money to someone you've only met online, no matter how convincing their story.

5. Investment and Ponzi Schemes:
These promise high returns with little or no risk. Ponzi schemes pay returns to earlier investors using money from new investors rather than from profit. Legitimate investments always involve some risk and are regulated by SEBI. Be wary of investment opportunities that pressure you to act quickly or recruit others.

6. Fake Government Official Scams:
Scammers impersonate tax officials, police officers, or other government authorities. They threaten legal action, arrest, or penalties unless immediate payment is made. Real government agencies send official notices by post and don't demand instant payment over phone. They never ask for payment in gift cards, cryptocurrencies, or wire transfers.

7. Online Shopping and E-commerce Fraud:
Fake e-commerce websites offer products at unrealistically low prices. After payment, customers receive counterfeit goods, incorrect items, or nothing at all. Always shop from trusted platforms, check seller ratings, use secure payment methods, and verify website authenticity before making purchases.

8. Rental and Real Estate Scams:
Fraudsters list properties they don't own on rental or real estate websites. They demand advance payments or security deposits and disappear after receiving money. Always verify property ownership, meet the landlord in person, visit the property before paying, and use legal documentation for transactions.
