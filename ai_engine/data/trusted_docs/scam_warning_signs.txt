Common Scam Warning Signs in India

Job Scams:
- Legitimate companies never ask for money upfront for job interviews or training.
- Be wary of job offers that promise unrealistically high salaries with minimal work.
- Genuine employers conduct interviews and background checks before hiring.
- Never pay registration fees, security deposits, or training fees to get a job.
- Verify the company's credentials on their official website and LinkedIn.

Phishing Scams:
- Banks and financial institutions never ask for passwords, PINs, or OTPs via SMS, email, or phone.
- Check the sender's email address carefully - scammers often use similar-looking domains.
- Hover over links before clicking to see the actual URL destination.
- Look for spelling errors and grammatical mistakes in official-looking messages.
- Government agencies and banks don't send urgent payment requests via email.

Investment Fraud:
- Be skeptical of investment schemes promising guaranteed high returns with zero risk.
- Ponzi schemes often pay early investors with money from new investors, not from actual profits.
- Legitimate investments are registered with SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India).
- Research any investment opportunity thoroughly before committing money.
- If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is a scam.

Online Shopping Scams:
- Use trusted payment methods that offer buyer protection.
- Check seller ratings and reviews on e-commerce platforms.
- Be cautious of deals that are significantly cheaper than market prices.
- Verify the website's security (HTTPS) and contact information.
- Save all transaction records and communication with sellers.
