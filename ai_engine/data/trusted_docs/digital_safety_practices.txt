Digital Safety Best Practices for Indian Users

General Online Safety:
- Create strong, unique passwords for each account using a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available for added security.
- Keep your operating system, browsers, and apps updated with the latest security patches.
- Be cautious about what personal information you share on social media.
- Use secure, password-protected WiFi networks and avoid public WiFi for sensitive transactions.

Financial Security:
- Never share your bank account details, credit card numbers, CVV, or OTPs with anyone.
- Monitor your bank statements regularly for unauthorized transactions.
- Use virtual payment addresses (UPI) instead of sharing account numbers.
- Enable transaction alerts via SMS and email from your bank.
- Set spending limits on cards and digital wallets.
- Report suspicious transactions to your bank immediately.

Social Engineering Awareness:
- Scammers create urgency to make you act without thinking - take time to verify.
- Government officials don't threaten arrests or demand immediate payment over phone.
- No legitimate organization will ask you to transfer money to personal accounts.
- Be suspicious of unsolicited calls, messages, or emails asking for personal information.
- Verify caller identity by calling back through official channels listed on the organization's website.

Protecting Your Digital Identity:
- Don't click on suspicious links in SMS, WhatsApp, or email messages.
- Verify QR codes before scanning, especially for payments.
- Keep your Aadhaar, PAN, and other ID documents secure and share only when absolutely necessary.
- Be careful about downloading apps from unknown sources.
- Report cyber fraud to cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (national cyber crime helpline).
